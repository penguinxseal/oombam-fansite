Chapter 01

Before OomBam

How Their Paths Crossed

Before OomBam became an official on-screen pairing, Oom Eisaya and Bam Saralee were already familiar with one another through their professional circles. Both were connected to Channel 3’s Duangjai Tewaprom universe, although they appeared in different installments and Fulfill would become their first official acting partnership as a lead pair.

Bam later explained that they had known each other from an earlier project and had already spent some time together before Fulfill. Their connection at that stage was still closer to that of colleagues: they talked, joined activities, went out together, and even made merit together, but the level of closeness audiences would later associate with OomBam had not yet formed.

That prior familiarity mattered when a new Girls’ Love project began taking shape. They were not being introduced as complete strangers. There was already enough comfort between them for Oom to imagine that Bam might be someone with whom she could build something deeper on screen.

Chapter 02

The Choice

How OomBam Was Formed

OomBam was not formed simply by placing two actresses together and asking them to create chemistry. Oom was involved in the Fulfill casting process first, while the production continued searching for the person who could match her naturally.

Oom recalled that the team had auditioned other candidates but had not yet found the combination they were looking for. When they asked whether there was someone she already felt comfortable with — someone she believed she could build chemistry with — Oom thought of Bam and suggested that she come in to audition.

Bam confirmed that Oom had wanted her to join the casting process and that they talked extensively before the audition. The production also experimented with their roles: Oom explained that the initial configuration did not feel right, but after they switched the parts, the team found the chemistry it had been searching for.

That casting decision became the professional beginning of OomBam. In Fulfill, Oom took the role of Ai-Oon and Bam played Pa-Fun, a married couple whose story begins after the wedding and explores how two people continue learning, communicating, and growing together.

Chapter 03

Finding Each Other

Building the Chemistry

Because Ai-Oon and Pa-Fun enter Fulfill as two women who already know and love each other deeply, Oom and Bam understood that believable chemistry could not be left to chance. Bam said they needed to become much closer and know each other as thoroughly as possible. Oom described the process as having to find both the characters and the chemistry between themselves.

They deliberately spent time getting to know each other beyond ordinary filming requirements. Oom brought Bam to additional acting workshops where communication exercises encouraged them to understand how the other person thinks, responds, and uses emotional energy. They also traveled, joined activities, ate together, and made merit together.

The result was a chemistry built through work rather than treated as an accident. By the time Fulfill premiered on 24 April 2026, the pairing was being introduced publicly as Oom and Bam’s first official collaboration and first Girls’ Love series together.

Audience interest grew quickly. After only two episodes had aired, they held the OomBam 1st Fan Meeting in Taipei on 3 May 2026, an early sign that the pairing was already reaching viewers beyond Thailand.

Chapter 04

Beyond the Characters

The Friendship Behind OomBam

The closeness required for Fulfill did not stay limited to rehearsals and scenes. In interviews, Oom and Bam spoke about how the process of preparing for the series made them know one another more deeply and become more comfortable together.

Their off-screen time included conversations, meals, outings, activities, workshops, and merit-making. Oom explained that understanding the kind of energy each of them uses helped them know each other better, while Bam spoke about the importance of communicating openly so they could understand one another’s perspective.

That is an important part of the OomBam story because it separates the pairing from the characters they portray. Ai-Oon and Pa-Fun are a fictional romantic couple. Oom and Bam are real people whose private lives remain their own. What can be documented is the friendship, trust, professional partnership, and growing familiarity that they themselves have publicly discussed.

As Fulfill continued, that shared public identity expanded through interviews, fan activities, performances, and official content. Their first official photobook, Blooming With You, was announced in June 2026 and was followed by their first OomBam photobook fansign in Bangkok on 11 July, creating another shared project outside the television episodes themselves.

Chapter 05

Still Becoming

Where Their Journey Stands Today

Fulfill reached its final episode on 12 June 2026, celebrated with the Fulfill Final Episode with OomBam event at SiamPic Hall. The event looked back on the series through talks, games, performances, fan projects, and a shared viewing of the finale — closing the drama while also showing that the OomBam partnership had already grown beyond the weekly broadcast.

The pairing continued through official projects and fan-facing activities. Their Blooming With You photobook and first Bangkok fansign followed in June and July, and a first fansign in Shanghai was announced for August. These activities gave OomBam an identity that was no longer tied only to Ai-Oon and Pa-Fun.

That is where the story stands now: Fulfill provided the project that formally brought Oom and Bam together as an on-screen pair, but the work of building trust, chemistry, and a shared public partnership continued beyond the final episode.

OomBam’s story is therefore still an unfolding one. It began with familiarity, became a deliberate casting choice, developed through workshops and time spent understanding each other, and found an audience through Fulfill. What follows — new work, new experiences, and whatever Oom and Bam themselves choose to share — becomes the next chapter.
