OOM × BAMA STORY IN BLOOM

Our Journey, Our Memories

“One story, many memories—and many more still to come.”

Chapter 01

Before OomBam

How Their Paths Crossed
◎Source: @oombam_ch3View original post on Instagram ↗

Before OomBam became an official on-screen pairing, Oom Eisaya and Bam Saralee already knew one another through their professional circles. Both were connected to Channel 3’s Duangjai Tewaprom universe, though in different installments, and Fulfill would become their first official acting partnership as a lead pair.

Bam later shared that they had spent time together before Fulfill—talking, joining activities, going out, and making merit—but were still closer to colleagues than the OomBam audiences would later know. That familiarity mattered when a new Girls’ Love project began taking shape: they were not starting as strangers, and there was already enough comfort for Oom to imagine building something deeper with Bam on screen.

Chapter 02

The Choice

How OomBam Was Formed
◎Source: @oombam_ch3View original post on Instagram ↗

OomBam was not formed simply by placing two actresses together and hoping for chemistry. Oom entered the Fulfill casting process first while the production continued searching for someone who could match her naturally.

When the team asked whether there was someone she already felt comfortable with, Oom thought of Bam and suggested that she audition. Bam later confirmed that Oom had encouraged her to join the process. The production also tested their roles; Oom explained that the first configuration did not feel right, but after they switched parts, the chemistry clicked. That decision became OomBam’s professional beginning: Oom as Ai-Oon and Bam as Pa-Fun, the married couple at the center of Fulfill.

Chapter 03

Finding Each Other

Building the Chemistry
◎Source: @oombam_ch3View original post on Instagram ↗

Ai-Oon and Pa-Fun begin Fulfill as two women who already know and love each other deeply, so Oom and Bam knew believable chemistry could not be left to chance. Bam said they needed to become much closer, while Oom described the process as finding both the characters and the chemistry between themselves.

They spent time together beyond ordinary filming requirements, including additional acting and communication workshops, meals, activities, travel, and merit-making. The chemistry audiences eventually saw was therefore something deliberately built through trust and work. Fulfill premiered on 24 April 2026, and after only two episodes, OomBam held their first fan meeting in Taipei on 3 May—an early sign that their partnership was already reaching viewers beyond Thailand.

Chapter 04

Beyond the Characters

The Friendship Behind OomBam
◎Source: @oomeisayaView original post on Instagram ↗

The closeness developed for Fulfill did not stay inside rehearsals and scenes. Oom and Bam have spoken publicly about becoming more comfortable with one another through conversations, meals, outings, workshops, activities, and merit-making. Oom described learning how each of them uses emotional energy, while Bam emphasized open communication and understanding each other’s perspective.

That distinction matters: Ai-Oon and Pa-Fun are fictional romantic characters, while Oom and Bam are real people whose private lives remain their own. What can be documented is the friendship, trust, professional partnership, and familiarity they have chosen to share publicly. That partnership expanded beyond the series through interviews, fan activities and their first official photobook, Blooming With You, followed by its Bangkok fansign in July 2026.

Chapter 05

Still Becoming

Where Their Journey Stands Today
◎Source: @oombam_ch3View original post on Instagram ↗

Fulfill reached its final episode on 12 June 2026, celebrated with the Fulfill Final Episode with OomBam event at SiamPic Hall. The drama ended, but the partnership had already grown beyond a weekly broadcast. Blooming With You and its Bangkok fansign followed, alongside further official and fan-facing activities that gave OomBam a shared identity beyond Ai-Oon and Pa-Fun.

That is where the journey stands: Fulfill formally brought Oom and Bam together as an on-screen pair, while the trust, chemistry, and public partnership they built continued after the finale. Their story began with familiarity, became a deliberate casting choice, and developed through the work of understanding one another. What follows—new projects, new experiences, and whatever they choose to share—belongs to the chapters still becoming.
