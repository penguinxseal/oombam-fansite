COMMUNITY BLOSSOMS

Community Guidelines

A space where love for OomBam grows with kindness. These guidelines help us protect Oom, Bam, fellow fans, creators, and the welcoming spirit of the community.

← Back to Community Blossoms
These guidelines are here to protect the warm, respectful atmosphere of Community Blossoms. They apply to the Blossom Wall, community chat, fan submissions, projects, and other community spaces hosted on the fansite.
01

Oom & Bam Come First

Support Oom and Bam with care. Celebrate their work, growth, achievements, and partnership without turning support into pressure, entitlement, or demands about how they should behave on or off camera.

02

Respect Boundaries & Privacy

Do not share private schedules, addresses, hotel details, flight information, private accounts, leaked material, or other information that was not intended for public circulation. Avoid speculation about private relationships, families, health, or personal matters.

03

No Fandom Fights

Community Blossoms is not a place for fan wars, harassment, baiting, dog-piling, or attacks on other artists, pairings, fandoms, or fans. Supporting OomBam never requires putting someone else down.

04

Let the Past Stay in the Past

Avoid repeatedly reviving old pairings, comparisons, rumors, or past fandom conflicts in ways that disrespect Oom, Bam, their current work, or other people. Keep the focus on the journey being celebrated here.

05

Keep Blossom Chat Kind

Friendly teasing and fangirl excitement are welcome; personal attacks are not. Do not use slurs, threats, sexual harassment, bullying, or targeted humiliation. Give people room to disagree without making the space hostile.

06

Verify Before Sharing

Clearly separate confirmed information from rumor, translation, interpretation, or personal opinion. When possible, link back to the original public source and avoid spreading unverified claims as fact.

07

Credit Creators

Respect photographers, artists, translators, editors, and other creators. Keep visible watermarks intact, follow stated repost rules, and include creator credit and a source link whenever available.

08

Letters & Blossom Wall

Messages should be supportive and appropriate for a public fan space. Do not submit invasive questions, demands, insults, sexually explicit material, personal contact details, or content intended to provoke conflict.

09

Projects & Fundraising

Community projects should be transparent about organizers, purpose, timelines, and use of funds. Do not imply that an unofficial project is endorsed by Oom, Bam, their management, Channel 3, or this fansite unless that endorsement is explicitly confirmed.

10

Protect Yourself Too

Do not post sensitive personal information about yourself or another fan. Use care when sharing names, contact information, payment details, tickets, travel plans, or anything that could expose someone to unwanted attention.

11

Moderation

Community moderators may decline, hide, edit for safety or formatting, or remove content that conflicts with these guidelines. Repeated or serious violations may result in access being limited. Moderation decisions should prioritize safety, privacy, fairness, and the welcoming character of the community.

Our Blossom Promise

We celebrate loudly, love kindly, protect boundaries, give credit, and remember that behind every account is a person. May this remain a place where support for OomBam can grow without losing respect for them, for one another, or for ourselves.
